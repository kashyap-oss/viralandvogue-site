FAQ

Okay but real talk…

Will I actually go viral?

Nobody can honestly promise a viral video. What we promise is consistent, high-quality content built on what's working right now — which is exactly how brands give themselves the best shot at blowing up.

How soon will I see results?

Your page will look better from week one. Real growth in followers and enquiries usually builds over 60–90 days of consistent posting — that's why monthly plans start at 3 months.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google from your own account, so you stay in control. Our fee covers strategy, creatives and management.

Who owns my accounts and content?

You do — always. Your pages, ad accounts, website and every piece of content we make for you are yours.

Do you work outside Surat?

Yes, with brands across India and abroad. Everything runs over calls and WhatsApp, and you never need to visit a studio.

Do I need to arrange a photoshoot?

No. Just send a few clear phone photos of your product and we turn them into studio-quality campaign visuals within 48 hours. Every visual is checked so your product looks exactly like what customers receive.

Can you make content in Gujarati or Hindi?

Absolutely. Local-language content often performs better because it feels real. We create in English, Gujarati and Hindi.
