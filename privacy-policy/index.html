Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Viral & Vogue ("we", "us") provides social media management services to businesses. This policy explains how our internal publishing tool, Viral & Vogue Publisher, handles data when it connects to Facebook and Instagram on behalf of our clients.

What the app does

The app is used only by our team to publish and schedule content on Facebook Pages and Instagram business accounts that our clients have authorised us to manage, and to read basic Page and post information needed to do that.

Information we access

  • Basic information about the Facebook Pages and Instagram business accounts we are authorised to manage (name, ID).
  • Content we create and publish for our clients (text, images, videos) and basic engagement figures for those posts.

The app does not collect personal data from the public, followers or visitors, and it does not use Facebook Login for members of the public.

How we use it

We use this information only to publish content and report results to the client who owns the account. We do not sell, rent or share it with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

Storage and security

Access tokens and content files are stored securely and accessed only by our team. We keep data only as long as we manage the client's account, or as required by law.

Data deletion

A client can remove our access at any time from their Meta Business settings. To ask us to delete any data related to your account, email us at viralandvogue@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.
